01

PFT (Pulmonary Function Testing)

PFT is referred to as a Lung Function Test. It is a crucial diagnostic tool to detect chest disorders like COPD, IPF, Chronic Cough, Asthma, etc.

02

Peakflometry

The Peakflometry is a tool to monitor the progress of airway diseases such as asthma and COPD. It is important equipment to level up or level down ongoing medication. Patients can use it on a regular basis on their own.

03

Pulse Oximetry

Pulse Oximetry is a simple non-invasive method of monitoring the oxygen saturation of Hemoglobin in the blood, which in most cases reflects the gas exchange capabilities of the Respiratory system. A test is provided to each patient for examination at each visit.

04

BiPAP / CPAP / O2 Therapy Rental

Non-invasive ventilation, such as CPAP or BiPAP, is used in patients with sleep disorders such as Sleep Apnea, severe stable COPD, and Obesity-Hypoventilation Syndrome.

05

Pulmonary Rehabilitation

Pulmonary Rehabilitation programs aim to improve the quality of life of a chronically ill patient, who often finds it difficult to perform activities of daily living. The program includes exercise training, nutrition counseling, meditation, Yoga asanas, etc.

06

Polysomnography

Nowadays a lot of attention is being paid to sleep medicine. With the increasing problem of sedentary lifestyles and obesity, sleep disorders are on the rise. Untreated sleep disorders have been linked to high blood pressure, heart disease, depression, diabetes, and other chronic diseases.

07

6MWT

The Six-Minute Walk Test (6MWT) is an easy to perform and practical test that has been used in the evaluation of patients with a variety of Cardiopulmonary diseases, including Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension (PAH). It simply measures the distance a patient can walk on a flat, hard surface in a period of 6 minutes.

08

Sputum Test

A Sputum test is conducted to identify and detect bacteria or fungi. These generally infect the lungs or respiratory tract. Sputum is a thick fluid that is found inside the lungs and surrounding airways. It is always preferred to have fresh morning samples tested for bacteriological examination of Sputum.

09

Vaccination

Vaccination is one of the best ways to prevent diseases and stay healthy. People with asthma or COPD are at a higher risk of serious problems from certain vaccine-preventable diseases. People diagnosed with lung diseases should consult their doctor on regular basis and should consider updating their vaccination.

Bronchoscopy

Seeing inside the airways

What is a Bronchoscopy?

Bronchoscopy is a way to examine airways. An instrument called a Bronchoscope is passed into a person’s airways through the nose or mouth. It goes inside to reach the lungs. The instrument has a light and a camera on its top end and is made of super-flexible fiber optic material.

Why does the doctor order a bronchoscopy?

The human respiratory system is made up of several things such as the Larynx, Trachea, Bronchi, and Bronchioles. A doctor using a Bronchoscope can see and examine all these structures.

Video Bronchoscopy

The Bronchoscope is attached to a camera on its end that displays video images of your Bronchus on a screen. Modern-day bronchoscopes are enabled with color videos that help doctors in documenting their findings.
